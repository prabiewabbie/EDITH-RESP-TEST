Put licensed or royalty-free music here (mp3/wav/m4a/flac).
Filename words are mood tags: epic, devotional, calm, tension, triumphant.
Add <track>.txt next to a track with its credit line; it goes into the YouTube description.
This repo is public: only commit tracks whose licence allows redistribution (CC0, public domain,
or CC BY with the credit in the .txt). YouTube Audio Library tracks may be used in your videos but
not redistributed, so keep those out of this repo.

With no tracks here, every reel gets an original tanpura-drone score for its mood
(gita_reels/score.py), which needs no credit.
