# Post 25 — style guide

Calm, contemplative, reverent short video for 'Echoes of Gita'. Master aspect 4:5, with 9:16 safe framing: keep key subjects centred with headroom top and bottom for captions added in editing. Two visual modes that share one palette. (1) Nature and modern-life scenes: photorealistic and cinematic, with soft natural light (morning gold, late-afternoon amber), shallow depth of field and slow, steady camera moves (gentle push-ins, drifts, overheads). (2) Scripture and devotional scenes: painterly and reverent, in the style of traditional Indian devotional painting with soft brushwork and luminous gilded light. Shared palette: lotus-leaf greens, deep teal water, blush and lotus pink, warm temple gold, soft ivory highlights, and muted desaturated greys for the office scenes. Mood: serene, unhurried, hopeful. Modern-life figures are always anonymous: seen from behind, in silhouette, or with faces out of focus, and never resembling real or famous people. No brands or logos anywhere. Divine figures are shown with dignity in traditional iconography. Absolutely no text, letters, captions, subtitles or watermarks in any generated frame; all on-screen text is added in editing. Pacing: slow dissolves between scenes, about 51 seconds in total.

## Scene 1 · video · 4:5

Cinematic macro shot of a broad, waxy green lotus leaf floating on a still pond in soft early-morning light. Clear water droplets bead into perfect silver spheres on the leaf's velvety surface and roll off its edge without wetting it, falling into the water with tiny ripples. 100mm macro lens, very shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh of reflected sunlight on the water. Slow push-in. Palette of fresh lotus green, deep teal water and pale gold highlights. Calm, meditative and photorealistic.

Negative: text, letters, captions, watermark, logo, people, hands, insects, murky colours, harsh midday light, fast motion, cartoon style, oversaturation, distortion

## Scene 0 · voiceover · 4:5

Warm, calm, gender-neutral adult narrator with a gentle Indian-English accent, speaking at an unhurried, reflective pace with soft pauses between sentences. Pronounce 'Bhagavad Gita' and 'Kurukshetra' correctly. Read the verse slightly slower, with quiet reverence. The tone is intimate and encouraging, never preachy. Script: Water rolls right off a lotus leaf. What if office politics could roll off you the same way? [pause] The Monday stand-up. A group chat that won't stop buzzing. A meeting where someone else takes the credit. [pause] In Chapter 5 of the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna describes a way to remain in action without being bound by it. [pause] Bhagavad Gita, chapter five, verse ten: He who acteth, placing all actions in the Eternal, abandoning attachment, is unaffected by sin as a lotus leaf by the waters. [pause] Notice that the verse doesn't ask us to leave our work. Commentators across traditions broadly read it as karma yoga: keep acting, offer the action to the Eternal, and let go of attachment. [pause] Here is a reflection, not scripture: do your part well and honestly. Offer it beyond your ego. Loosen your grip on recognition and on how things turn out. [pause] You can stay in the pond without soaking up its mud. [pause] So, a gentle question for tomorrow: what would change if you worked like a lotus leaf?

Negative: celebrity voice imitation, real person voice clone, dramatic movie-trailer tone, shouting, rushed pacing, comedic delivery, background noise

## Scene 0 · music · 4:5
