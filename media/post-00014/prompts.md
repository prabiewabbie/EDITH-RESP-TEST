# Post 14 — style guide

Unified painterly digital style with soft brush texture, fine film grain and gentle atmospheric depth, consistent across all 7 slides. Night-world slides (1, 4, 5, 6) use deep navy, indigo and muted teal with cool screen-glow accents. Guna slides each have a signature palette: Sattva in soft white, pale gold and gentle blue; Rajas in warm red, orange and ember against darkness; Tamas in heavy grey-blue haze. Sacred and resolution slides (2, 7) use warm gold, saffron and amber, with Slide 2 rendered in a reverent traditional Indian miniature-inspired style. Lighting is soft and directional with no harsh contrast. Compositions are calm and centred, with generous negative space (top third or bottom band) reserved for on-screen text added in editing. Mood is quiet, contemplative, compassionate and non-judgmental. No text, letters, numerals, logos or UI anywhere in the generated images. Human presence is anonymous only: silhouettes, never faces. Divine figures are always shown with dignity in traditional iconography. Master aspect is 4:5, with safe margins so each image can be recropped to 9:16.
