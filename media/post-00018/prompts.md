# Post 18 — style guide

Calm, contemplative, cinematic look for a 50-second devotional short. Master aspect 4:5, with safe framing that crops cleanly to 9:16 (keep key subjects in the central vertical third and leave negative space top and bottom for captions added in editing). Palette: warm amber and honey tones for the indoor and dusk scenes, moving into deep ocean blues, soft teal, misty whites and golden-hour highlights. Krishna's blue and Arjuna's saffron and gold appear only in the scripture scene. Lighting is soft, natural and directional (evening lamp glow, golden dawn, golden hour), with gentle haze and no harsh contrast. Camera work is slow and meditative: slow push-ins, gentle drone glides, long dissolves, no frantic cuts and no shaky cam. Lens feel is 50-85mm with shallow depth of field for close-ups and 24-35mm for wide landscapes. The modern scenes are photoreal with a slight film grain. The scripture scene is a painterly, reverent rendering in traditional Indian devotional iconography (Raja Ravi Varma-inspired oil-painting texture) that blends smoothly with the photoreal ocean imagery. The mood moves from mild restlessness to steadiness and peace. Absolutely no text, letters, logos, watermarks or brand marks in any generated visual; all on-screen text, the verse, the citation and the Echoes of Gita logo are added in editing.

## Scene 0 · voiceover · 4:5

Warm, calm, gender-neutral adult narrator with a gentle Indian-English accent, an unhurried pace, a soft and intimate tone like a trusted friend, and slight reverence on scripture lines. Leave natural pauses between scenes. Deliver the quoted verse slowly and with dignity. Script: "Your phone may have buzzed dozens of times today, each time with something you suddenly 'need.' [pause] Flash sales. Limited-time offers. A reminder that your cart is waiting. Each one asks for a little more of your attention. [pause] In the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna describes the person of steady wisdom using an image from nature. [pause] Rivers pour into the ocean, day and night. The ocean is already full, and it stays steady. [pause] He attaineth Peace, into whom all desires flow as rivers flow into the ocean, which is filled with water, but remaineth unmoved—not he who desireth desires. [long pause] The verse doesn't condemn wanting things, or buying them. Commentators broadly read it as the difference between desires arising, and being pulled around by every one of them. [pause] A reflection, not scripture: the offers will keep flowing in. Let them come. Today, can you stay the ocean?"

Negative: robotic tone, rushed delivery, overly dramatic or theatrical voice, sales-pitch energy, celebrity voice imitation, background noise, mispronunciation

## Scene 0 · music · 4:5

Mixed low to sit beneath the narration, with no percussion hits and no vocals. Mood: from mild unrest to deep steadiness and peace.

Negative: lyrics, vocals, heavy drums, EDM drops, aggressive build-ups, cinematic trailer hits, comedic tones, copyrighted melodies, abrupt endings
