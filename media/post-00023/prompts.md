# Post 23 — style guide

Two visual worlds that share one palette and one quiet mood. MODERN BEDROOM (scenes 1, 5, 6, 7): photorealistic cinematic look, 35mm and 50mm lenses, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, slow handheld-stable or locked-off camera. The light moves from cold phone-blue (#2B4A6F) toward warm amber diya and lamplight (#E8A94B). No faces are ever shown, and no readable text, logos, flags or political imagery appears. SACRED WORLD (scenes 2, 3, 4): painterly Indian devotional art in the spirit of traditional miniature and temple painting, with soft brush texture, luminous gold leaf highlights and gentle atmospheric haze. Krishna always appears in traditional iconography: blue complexion, peacock feather crown, yellow pitambara garments, serene and kind. Arjuna is dignified, in warrior attire with his bow lowered. The palette is dawn gold (#F2C46D), soft sky blue (#8FB8DE), ivory and deep indigo. Both worlds share calm pacing, reverent stillness and slow push-ins or drifts, with no fast cuts. The master aspect is 4:5, with safe-area composition for a 9:16 reframe (subjects kept centred, generous headroom). All on-screen text is added in editing, so frames contain no text.

## Scene 0 · voiceover · 4:5

Warm, calm, gender-neutral adult voice with a soft Indian English accent. Intimate late-night delivery, unhurried, near-whisper but clear, with gentle pauses between scenes. Read the quoted verse slowly and reverently. Script: "It's 1 a.m. You've seen too much of the world tonight. Even Arjuna once asked to see something gentler. [pause] In Chapter 11 of the Bhagavad Gita, on the field of Kurukshetra, Arjuna asks Krishna to reveal His universal form, and Krishna grants it. [pause] The form is vast and radiant, beyond measure. Arjuna is shaken by it, and he asks Krishna to show again His familiar form. [pause] And when Krishna does, Arjuna says: 'Beholding again Thy gentle human Form, O Janârdana, I am now collected, and am restored to my own nature.' [pause] A reflection, not scripture: commentators read this moment in different ways. Some see the closeness of friendship. Others see the human mind before the infinite. Either way, Arjuna finds his footing again. [pause] When the world feels too vast, you might notice what helps you feel collected again. A familiar face. A quiet prayer. A few slow breaths. [pause] Not to look away from the world, but to return to steadiness. Rest well." Pronounce Janârdana as jah-NAAR-duh-nuh and Kurukshetra as koo-rook-SHAY-truh.

Negative: dramatic movie-trailer tone, shouting, robotic cadence, fast pace, sing-song delivery, celebrity voice imitation, background noise
