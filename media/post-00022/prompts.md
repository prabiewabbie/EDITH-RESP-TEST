# Post 22 — style guide

Seven-image contemplative carousel with a visual arc from scattered restlessness to steady calm. Two blended treatments: cinematic photorealism for modern scenes (1, 4, 5, 7) and soft painterly realism, like gouache or oil with visible brushwork, for symbolic and scriptural scenes (2, 3, 6). Palette: cool desaturated blues, slate and indigo carry restlessness. Warm amber, brass and saffron-gold carry steadiness. Each image keeps one small warm accent, building to a fully warm final frame. Lighting is low-key and natural, with gentle falloff, soft film grain and no harsh contrast. Composition: 4:5 master with clean negative space in the lower third so on-screen text can be added in editing, keeping the main subject in the upper two-thirds. The subject stays centred enough to survive a 9:16 reframe. Mood is quiet, reverent and introspective, never dramatic or bleak. No text, letters, logos or brand marks appear in any image. Modern people are anonymous: no faces, only hands or figures seen from behind. Divine figures appear only in dignified traditional iconography.

## Scene 0 · image · 4:5

Cinematic photorealistic night interior. A dim home desk is lit mainly by the cool blue-white glow of an open laptop. The laptop screen shows a crowded strip of many tiny browser tabs rendered as abstract blurred shapes with no readable content. A smartphone lies beside it, its screen glowing with soft, unreadable blurred notification shapes. At the frame edge, anonymous hands press against a forehead; the face is cropped out and not visible. A cold, untouched cup of tea, a few blank scattered sticky notes and a tangled charging cable sit on the desk. Behind, a window shows a quiet city at night with soft bokeh lights. One small warm lamp glows far in the background. Shot on a 35mm lens at f/2, slightly high angle, shallow depth of field. Muted blue and slate palette, mood of restless fatigue. Clean dark negative space in the lower third.

Negative: text, letters, words, readable screen content, logos, brand names, app icons, watermark, visible face, recognisable person, celebrity likeness, political symbols, bright cheerful lighting, cartoon, distorted hands, extra fingers

## Scene 1 · image · 4:5

Reverent painterly scene in traditional Indian devotional iconography, in the style of classical Pahari and Raja Ravi Varma inspired oil painting. Krishna has a serene blue complexion and wears a peacock-feather crown, yellow silk pitambara, gold ornaments and a flower garland. He sits as charioteer at the front of an ornate wooden chariot drawn by white horses, turning gently toward Arjuna with a calm, compassionate expression and one hand raised in a teaching gesture. Arjuna, a dignified warrior prince in traditional attire and crown, sits in the chariot listening attentively with hands folded in respect, his bow resting beside him. Soft golden dawn light spreads over the open plain of Kurukshetra. Distant armies appear only as faint, hazy silhouettes and small banners on the far horizon, peaceful and still. Medium-wide composition at eye level with figures in the upper two-thirds. The lower third holds open warm parchment-toned ground and haze as clean negative space. Palette of saffron, gold, soft blue and cream, with a mood of sacred calm.

Negative: text, letters, script, watermark, logos, sexualised depiction, revealing or suggestive poses, comedic, cartoonish, caricature, meme style, chibi, exaggerated expressions, violence, gore, blood, weapons in use, injury, combat, corpses, modern clothing, real living people, celebrity likeness, political symbols, flags of nations, distorted anatomy, extra limbs, disrespectful depiction of divine figures

## Scene 2 · image · 4:5

Painterly close-up of dry autumn leaves scattered across a weathered grey stone courtyard. A gusty wind blows the leaves in many different directions, some lifted mid-air with soft motion blur and others skidding along the stones. Soft, diffuse overcast light. Low camera angle near the ground, 50mm lens feel, leaves sweeping across the upper and middle frame, calmer bare stone in the lower third. Earthy browns, ochre and muted gold against cool grey stone. The mood suggests motion without destination and gentle unrest.

Negative: text, letters, watermark, logos, people, faces, bright saturated colours, harsh sunlight, cartoon, clutter, buildings dominating frame

## Scene 3 · image · 4:5

Cinematic photorealistic close-up of a small traditional brass oil lamp (diya) on a dark wooden windowsill. Its single flame flickers and bends sharply sideways in a draught from a partly open window, and a faint wisp of smoke trails off. The background is deep, near-black with a hint of cool blue night beyond the window. Warm amber light from the flame falls softly on the brass and the sill. Shot on a 85mm macro lens with shallow depth of field. The flame sits in the upper-middle frame, with dark negative space below. The mood is of attention that cannot settle.

Negative: text, letters, watermark, logos, people, hands, multiple lamps, fire hazard, large fire, steady upright flame, bright room, cartoon, religious symbols with text

## Scene 4 · image · 4:5

Photorealistic wide landscape of a remote mountain lake at dusk. Wind ruffles the water surface into choppy ripples, so the reflection of the surrounding snow-dusted peaks is broken into scattered fragments of light. Low, moving clouds hang over the summits. Cool blue, slate and soft violet tones, with a faint last glow on the horizon. Shot on a 24mm lens with a balanced horizon in the upper third, and the fragmented water reflection filling the middle and lower frame. There are no people. The mood is unsettled stillness.

Negative: text, letters, watermark, logos, people, boats, buildings, mirror-calm water, clear perfect reflection, oversaturated colours, harsh sunset orange, cartoon

## Scene 5 · image · 4:5

Painterly night scene of an anonymous person seen from behind, sitting alone on a simple balcony chair with shoulders slightly slumped. They wear plain, casual, unbranded clothing and look out over scattered warm city lights. Above is a deep indigo sky with a few faint stars. A small potted plant sits beside the railing. A soft rim light falls from the city glow, and the face is not visible. The figure sits in the middle frame, slightly off-centre, with expansive sky above and a darker balcony floor in the lower third. Indigo and navy palette with tiny amber city lights. The mood is gentle, contemplative and quietly searching, not despairing.

Negative: text, letters, signage, billboards, logos, watermark, visible face, recognisable person, celebrity likeness, political symbols, despair, crying, dramatic tragedy, dangerous pose near edge, cartoon, distorted anatomy

## Scene 6 · image · 4:5

Warm cinematic photorealistic still life on a clean wooden desk. A closed laptop and a smartphone placed face down sit neatly to one side. A single small brass diya burns with a perfectly steady, upright, calm flame beside a small ceramic cup of warm tea with a faint curl of steam. The space is uncluttered. Behind, a window shows a still mountain lake at twilight, its mirror-smooth surface reflecting the peaks clearly. Soft golden lamplight with gentle shadows. Shot on a 50mm lens at eye level with shallow depth of field, objects in the upper-middle frame and open warm desk surface in the lower third. Amber, honey-gold and soft blue twilight palette. The mood is calm, inviting and gathered.

Negative: text, letters, logos, brand names, visible screen content, watermark, people, faces, clutter, flickering or bent flame, choppy water, harsh lighting, cartoon
