# Post 9 — style guide

Cinematic, contemplative short video for 'Echoes of Gita', master 4:5 (keep key subjects centered in a safe zone for 9:16 reframing). Two visual registers that share one palette: (1) Home and nursery scenes: photorealistic or lightly painterly, shallow depth of field, 35mm and 50mm lens look, slow dolly and drift moves, warm tungsten lamp glow (2700K) mixed with soft blue moonlight or dawn light, gentle film grain. (2) Scriptural scenes: traditional Indian miniature-painting style (Pahari/Kangra influence), soft golden light, fine brushwork, flat-perspective landscapes, gold leaf accents. Shared palette: saffron, warm gold, deep indigo, muted teal-blue, cream, brass. Mood: tender, quiet, reverent, unhurried, compassionate. Parents are never identifiable (faces in shadow, turned away or out of focus). Krishna and Arjuna appear only in dignified traditional iconography. No text, letters, numerals or logos in any generated frame; all on-screen text, verse lettering and branding are added in editing. Transitions are slow dissolves; a recurring brass diya motif links scenes 1, 4 and 6.

## Scene 1 · video · 4:5

Painterly-cinematic interior at dawn: a small wooden home altar against a cream wall, a brass diya unlit, a few dried marigold petals, a folded indigo meditation cushion beside it with a faint layer of dust visible in a shaft of pale window light. Warm low light, soft blue pre-dawn tones from the window. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, slow lateral camera drift past the altar toward a dim hallway where a faint warm nightlight glows at the far end. No people. Palette of brass, cream, indigo, muted gold. Quiet, wistful, still mood, gentle film grain.

Negative: text, letters, numerals, writing, captions, logos, watermark, people, faces, deity images shown disrespectfully, sexualised, comedic, cartoonish, meme-style, celebrity likeness, real living people, political symbols, flags, harsh light, clutter, oversaturated colors

## Scene 0 · voiceover · 4:5

Warm, gentle, unhurried narrator voice, adult, soft-spoken and compassionate, like speaking quietly to a tired friend at night; neutral Indian-English or neutral international English accent; slight reverence when quoting scripture, slower pace and soft pauses around the verse. Script, with pauses between scenes: 'You used to sit for meditation every morning. Now you can't remember the last time you slept four hours straight. ... Maybe you quietly feel you've fallen off the path. In Chapter 12 of the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna speaks to that kind of worry. ... Krishna offers Arjuna a series of paths, one after another. Fix your mind on Me. If not that, steady practice. If not that, work done for My sake. And then, one more. ... "If even to do this thou hast not strength, then, taking refuge in union with Me, renounce all fruit of action with the self controlled." ... Commentators across traditions have often read this sequence as an act of compassion: a path offered for every capacity, with no one shut out. ... As a reflection, not scripture: perhaps this season is not a failure of devotion. The feeding, the rocking, the care no one sees, can be offered without clinging to outcome or recognition. ... The cushion will wait. Tonight, you can simply rest in the care you're already giving.'

Negative: dramatic movie-trailer delivery, sing-song tone, rushed pacing, sarcasm, comedic inflection, celebrity voice imitation, background noise, reverb-heavy echo

## Scene 0 · music · 4:5

Soft, meditative ambient score, about 55 seconds, 60-66 BPM, in a calm major-leaning raga feel (evening/dawn mood such as Yaman or Bhairav-inspired, gentle). Begins with a low tanpura drone and faint rain-like texture; a sparse solo bansuri melody enters around scene 3, with subtle swarmandal shimmer under the verse (scene 4) and the volume dipping to leave space for narration. Warm felt-piano or soft pad swells gently at the riverbank scene, then resolves into a quiet, hopeful tonic with a lingering bansuri note and fade-out on the final gold frame. Intimate, tender, reverent, lullaby-like, never overpowering the voice.

Negative: percussion-heavy beats, drums, EDM, trailer hits, epic orchestral swells, vocals, lyrics, chanting of mantras out of context, dissonance, abrupt changes, comedic or upbeat pop tone
