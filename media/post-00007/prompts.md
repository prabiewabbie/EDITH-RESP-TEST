# Post 7 — style guide

Two visual worlds joined by one calm, contemplative tone. (1) Modern bedroom scenes (1, 2, 3, 7, 8): cinematic photorealism, 35mm and 50mm lenses, shallow depth of field, low-key lighting. The palette is deep indigo shadows, cool smartphone blue (#4A7BD0) and pale moonlight, with occasional warm amber accents. Fine film grain and slow, steady camera moves. Anonymous hands only: no faces, no identifiable people, no brand logos, no readable screens. (2) Gita scenes (4, 5, 6): traditional Indian miniature painting style (Pahari/Kangra-inspired) on a soft parchment texture. Fine linework, a gentle dawn gold glow (#D9A441), saffron, peacock blue and soft ivory, with a flat yet luminous perspective. Krishna and Arjuna appear in dignified traditional iconography. Armies only as distant misty silhouettes; no gore. Transitions are slow dissolves. Overall mood is quiet, gentle and non-judgmental. Absolutely no text, letters, numerals, scripts or logos in generated visuals; all on-screen text, the verse and the logo are added in editing. Master aspect 4:5, with a safe-area composition that also crops cleanly to 9:16.
