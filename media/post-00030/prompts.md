# Post 30 — style guide

Quiet, contemplative late-night-to-dawn visual arc. Cinematic painterly realism with soft film grain and gentle gouache-like texture, so photoreal and painterly frames feel like one series. Core palette: deep indigo, charcoal and midnight blue shadows with warm amber and brass-gold light accents (screen glow is cool blue, lamp and diya light is warm gold). The final frame shifts to soft rose and pale gold dawn. Lighting is low-key and single-source: phone glow, oil lamp or diya, or first daylight, with soft falloff and no harsh highlights. Compositions are calm and uncluttered, with generous negative space in the upper or lower third for text added in editing. People are anonymous: seen from behind, hands only, or distant silhouettes, never identifiable. The one sacred scene uses classical Indian miniature painting conventions (flat perspective, fine linework, mineral pigments, ornamental border-free framing) while keeping the same muted indigo-and-gold harmony. Mood throughout is compassionate, never dramatic or shaming. No text, letters, numerals, logos or UI anywhere in the generated images. Master aspect 4:5, with safe crops for 9:16.
