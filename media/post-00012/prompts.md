# Post 12 — style guide

Two visual worlds joined by soft dissolves. (1) MODERN: cinematic photorealism, small modern apartment in soft morning window light. 50mm and 85mm lenses, shallow depth of field, gentle handheld-stable movement. Warm muted palette of oat, sand, soft terracotta, sage and pale daylight, with low contrast and fine film grain. The protagonist is one fictional young adult who appears consistently across scenes: mid-20s, gender-neutral styling, shoulder-length dark hair loosely tied, oatmeal knit sweater, sage cotton trousers, cross-legged on a round rust-colored cushion. They must not resemble any real person. (2) SACRED: painterly animation in the style of a classical Indian miniature (Pahari/Kangra influence) gently brought to life. Flat-perspective landscape, fine brushwork, mineral pigments of lapis blue, saffron yellow, vermilion, leaf green and burnished gold, with soft parallax and slow reverent camera moves. Krishna is always blue-complexioned and serene, wearing yellow silk (pitambara) and a crown with a peacock feather, seated as charioteer. Arjuna always wears traditional warrior attire with a crown, his bow lowered and at rest, and an earnest expression. Both figures are depicted with dignity in traditional iconography. Shared motif: wind moving fabric, grass, curtains and flame. The mood is calm, honest, gently hopeful. Master aspect 4:5, with the central subject kept within a safe area so it can be reframed to 9:16. Never render text, numerals, letters, captions or logos in the frame; all on-screen text and the logo are added in editing.
