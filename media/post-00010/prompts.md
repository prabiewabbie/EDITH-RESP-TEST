# Post 10 — style guide

Quiet, contemplative devotional-cinematic look tying modern domestic realism to classical Indian painterly reverence. Master aspect 4:5 (reframe safely to 9:16; keep key subjects centered with generous headroom and footroom). Palette: warm honey-gold, soft saffron, brass, muted teak brown, cream and ivory, with gentle dawn blue in the shadows. Light: low-angle early-morning sunlight, soft volumetric beams, warm diya and candle glow, no harsh contrast. Camera: slow, steady moves (gentle push-ins, locked-off frames), shallow depth of field, 35mm-50mm equivalent for interiors, 85mm for close-ups, subtle film grain, soft halation on highlights. Modern scenes are photorealistic. The Krishna-Arjuna scene is a reverent painterly rendering in the tradition of classical Indian miniature and temple painting, harmonized to the same golden palette. Mood: calm, hopeful, non-judgmental. All human figures are anonymous, never real or identifiable people. No on-screen text, letters, numerals, logos or watermarks in any generated frame; all text, citations and branding are added in editing. Leave clean negative space in the upper and lower thirds for captions.
