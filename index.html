Echoes of Gita Studio

Short animated reels that walk through the Bhagavad Gita, one episode at a time.

Echoes of Gita Studio is a small personal tool that produces short vertical videos retelling the Bhagavad Gita in order, from chapter 1 to chapter 18, and uploads them to the Echoes of Gita YouTube channel.

Verse text comes only from a published translation and is checked word for word. Visuals are AI-generated and every video is labelled as such.

The tool uploads only to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
