Privacy Policy

Echoes of Gita Studio · Last updated 8 October 2026

Who this covers

Echoes of Gita Studio ("the Studio") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Echoes of Gita YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner, to manage that one channel. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

YouTube API Services

The Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using the Studio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the Studio accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the channel owner's own Google account, granted with the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes.
  • The channel's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments count, watch time), used to plan future episodes.

The Studio does not access, collect or store data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to upload the channel's own videos and to review their performance. OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub Actions settings and are never written to code or logs. Video statistics are kept in the Studio's own database for up to 12 months and then deleted. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings. When access is revoked, stored tokens stop working and are deleted, and stored statistics are deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Echoes of Gita channel page.
