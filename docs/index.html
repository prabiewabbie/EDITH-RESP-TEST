Echoes of Gita

Time stopped on the battlefield of Kurukshetra. The words spoken there never did.

Echoes of Gita is a YouTube channel of short films about the Bhagavad Gita. Each film pairs a verse, quoted word for word from Kashinath Telang's public domain 1882 translation, with a modern story that echoes it.

Echoes of Gita Studio is the private tool the channel's owner uses to write, check and publish those films. It is not offered to other users.
